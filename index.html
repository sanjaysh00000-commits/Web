Our Story & Ambiance

At Shanti Deep, we bring you the authentic taste of North India, with recipes passed down through three generations. We use only the freshest, locally sourced ingredients to prepare every dish, ensuring rich flavors and uncompromised quality.

Our warm, inviting ambiance is perfect for family dinners, group outings, or a quiet meal with friends. Whether you're a local resident or a traveler passing through Alipur Gijhauri, we welcome you with open arms and the promise of a memorable dining experience.
